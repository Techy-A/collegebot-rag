THAPAR INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY (TIET), PATIALA
PLACEMENTS AND RECRUITERS
Reference document compiled from public web sources (July 2026)

IMPORTANT: Placement figures are year-specific. Each number below is labelled
with the placement year it refers to. Do not treat a figure from one year as the
current figure for another.

AVERAGE PACKAGE (2025 PLACEMENT CYCLE)
  - Overall average package: Rs 11.38 LPA (lakh per annum).
  - The 2025 placement cycle was still in progress at the time these figures were
    published, so the average is an in-progress figure.
  - Branch-wise, Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) led with an average
    package of Rs 16.50 LPA.

HIGHEST PACKAGE
  - 2024 placement cycle: the highest package reached Rs 1.23 crore per annum,
    offered to a B.Tech CSE student by a leading technology company.
  - For the academic year 2024-25 cycle, placement was still under process and a
    final highest package had not been reported.
  - A separately reported figure lists a highest package of Rs 60 LPA for the 2026
    cycle; figures differ between reporting portals and between domestic and
    international offers, so verify against the official TIET placement report.

TOP RECRUITERS
Companies reported as participating in TIET recruitment include:
  - IBM
  - Oracle
  - Amazon Web Services (AWS)
  - Morgan Stanley
  - McKinsey & Company
  - KPMG
  - EY (Ernst & Young)
  - Mercer
  - Bosch Limited
  - Maruti Suzuki
  - Dabur
  - MakeMyTrip (MMT)

BRANCH-WISE PLACEMENT PERFORMANCE (2025)
In the 2025 cycle, B.Tech CSE recorded the highest number of offers and the
strongest packages, followed by Electronics and then Mechanical.

RANKINGS CONTEXT
TIET's NIRF 2025 rankings — Engineering rank 29, Research Institutions rank 42,
Overall rank 44 — provide useful context alongside placement outcomes. See the
overview document for detail.

WHERE TO VERIFY
Official placement statistics are published by the institute's Career Services /
Training and Placement office. Third-party education portals often report
differing figures for the same year, so the official TIET placement report is the
authoritative source.
