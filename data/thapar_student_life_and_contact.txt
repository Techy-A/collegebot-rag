SATURNALIA — ANNUAL TECHNO-CULTURAL FESTIVAL
Saturnalia is the premier annual cultural and technical festival of TIET Patiala.
  - The 2025 edition marked the festival's 50th anniversary.
  - It was held from 13 to 16 November 2025.
  - The edition featured over 100 events and expected more than 35,000 attendees.
  - Festival website: saturnalia.in
  - Festival contact: saturnalia [at] thapar [dot] edu

STUDENT SOCIETIES, CLUBS AND CHAPTERS
TIET hosts a wide range of student societies, professional chapters and clubs,
including:

  Professional and technical chapters:
    - ACM Computing Chapter
    - AIChE Student Chapter (American Institute of Chemical Engineers)
    - IIChE (Indian Institute of Chemical Engineers)
    - ASHRAE
    - IETE (Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers)
    - IET (Institution of Engineering and Technology)
    - IEI (Institution of Engineers India)
    - ISTE (Indian Society for Technical Education)
    - SAE (Society of Automotive Engineers)
    - Microsoft Learn Student Chapter / Microsoft Student Chapter
    - OWASP Student Chapter
    - Backslash Computing Society

  Cultural, service and general societies:
    - Youth Welfare Club (YWC)
    - MUDRA (Music and Dramatic Society)
    - Fine Arts and Photography Society (FAPS)
    - Kalam Society
    - Rotaract Club
    - AIESEC - Patiala Chapter
    - TEDx
    - Youth United - Thapar Chapter
    - Thapar Adventure Club

The official, current list of societies, clubs and student chapters is published
at https://www.thapar.edu/students/pages/student-societies (with a separate
listing for the Dera Bassi campus).

SPORTS AND EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES
Students participate in cricket, football, basketball, hockey, volleyball, lawn
tennis, table tennis and badminton, using the campus playgrounds and indoor
arenas. Extracurricular activity information is published at
https://www.thapar.edu/students/pages/extra-curricular
