THAPAR INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY (TIET), PATIALA
RESEARCH, CENTRES OF EXCELLENCE AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP
Reference document compiled from public web sources (July 2026)

RESEARCH THRUST AREAS
TIET's research spans the following areas:
  - Emerging materials
  - Artificial intelligence
  - Biomedical engineering
  - Smart manufacturing
  - Food security
  - Electric vehicles
  - Water resources management

RESEARCH OUTPUT
  - Over 1,500 research publications per year.
  - More than 5,000 research papers published in reputed international journals
    (cumulative).
  - 250+ patents held by the institute.

CENTRES OF EXCELLENCE
The institute houses 15 dedicated research centres. Named centres include:
  - Center for Advanced Materials Research
  - Artificial Intelligence & Robotics Lab
  - Water Resources Management Center
The official list of centres of excellence and funded projects is published at
https://thapar.edu/researches

INTERNATIONAL RESEARCH COLLABORATIONS
TIET participates in international research collaborations including partnerships

  - Trinity College Dublin (Ireland)

  - Virginia Tech (USA)

INCUBATION AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP
TIET runs several distinct entrepreneurship and incubation initiatives:

1. Science and Technology Entrepreneur's Park (STEP)
   A centre for business incubation at TIET that acts as a nursery for
   entrepreneurs and a launch pad for MSMEs. STEP has incubated 25 companies, of
   which 10 have gone on to start their own ventures.

2. Venture Lab Thapar
   An accelerator initiative for early-stage startups. Each year, 100+
   early-stage startups are expected to benefit from Venture Lab Thapar's
   programmes.

3. Thapar Entrepreneurship Centre
   Supports start-up ventures and nurtures the on-campus entrepreneurial
   ecosystem through hackathons, incubation programmes and industry-academia
   partnerships aimed at practical problem-solving.

4. Thapar Innovate
   The university's umbrella initiative that integrates research, innovation,
   incubation and funding under one roof, giving startups coordinated access to
   institutional support.
