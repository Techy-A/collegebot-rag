THAPAR INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY (TIET), PATIALA
ADMISSIONS AND ELIGIBILITY
Reference document compiled from public web sources (July 2026)

B.E. / B.TECH ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
A candidate is eligible for B.E./B.Tech admission if they have secured at least
60% marks in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in the Class 12 board examination.
The relaxed requirement for SC/ST candidates is 55% marks.

Students who have passed, or are appearing in, the 10+2 or equivalent examination
in 2024, 2025 or 2026 are eligible, provided they studied Physics and Mathematics
along with one of: Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, or a Technical Vocational
subject.

TWO MODES OF B.TECH ADMISSION
TIET admits B.E./B.Tech students through two parallel channels:
  1. JEE (Main) score channel — approximately 50% of seats are filled on the
     basis of the candidate's All India Rank in JEE Main.
  2. 10+2 merit channel — approximately the remaining 50% of seats are filled on
     the basis of the aggregate marks secured in Physics, Chemistry and
     Mathematics in the Class 12 board examination, combined with the TIET
     entrance route.

INTEGRATED ENGINEERING PROGRAMME (IEP)
Admission to the undergraduate IEP programme is based purely on merit in the
JEE (Main) examination, subject to meeting the 60% eligibility requirement in the
10+2 examination or equivalent.

APPLICATION AND COUNSELLING PROCESS
  - Candidates apply online through the official admissions portal
    (admissions.thapar.edu).
  - A merit list is prepared from the applicable channel (JEE Main rank or 10+2
    PCM aggregate).
  - Shortlisted candidates are invited to counselling. For the 2026 cycle,
    counselling is conducted in both online and offline (in-person) modes.
  - Counselling begins after the merit list is released. The B.E./B.Tech
    counselling schedule is published on the official website once the JEE Main
    or 10+2 results are declared.
  - Seat allotment follows the counselling rounds.

CUTOFF INFORMATION (JEE MAIN, ROUND 1, 2026, GENERAL CATEGORY)
Reported Round 1 cutoff ranges for the General category:
  - Home State (Punjab):  ranks from approximately 98,314 to 5,50,898
  - Other State:          ranks from approximately 53,883 to 1,71,351
Cutoffs vary substantially by branch — Computer Science and Engineering closes at
a much sharper rank than the wider range above. Cutoffs also shift between
counselling rounds and between years.

POSTGRADUATE AND DOCTORAL ADMISSION
M.E./M.Tech, M.Sc., MBA, MCA and PhD admissions run on separate notifications
with their own eligibility criteria and selection processes (which may include
GATE/CAT/institute-level tests and interviews depending on the programme).
Applicants should consult the specific programme notification for the relevant
intake.
