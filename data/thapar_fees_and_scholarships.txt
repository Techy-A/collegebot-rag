THAPAR INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY (TIET), PATIALA
FEE STRUCTURE AND SCHOLARSHIPS
Reference document compiled from public web sources (July 2026)

IMPORTANT: Fees are revised every academic year. The figures below are the
publicly reported figures for the 2026 admission cycle. Always verify against the
official fee circular at https://www.thapar.edu/students/pages/fee-circular-and-fee-chart
before making any payment or financial decision.

B.TECH TUITION FEE
  - Tuition fee for the full four-year B.Tech programme: approximately
    Rs 17,24,000 (seventeen lakh twenty-four thousand rupees).
  - Tuition is payable per semester: approximately Rs 2,15,500 per semester.
  - This tuition figure EXCLUDES development charges, hostel fees and mess
    expenses.

TOTAL PROGRAMME COST (B.TECH)
The total B.Tech programme cost for the 2026-30 batch is reported at
approximately Rs 25 lakh across the four-year programme, once development and
other charges are included. Hostel and mess costs add a further Rs 1.5 lakh to
Rs 2 lakh per year on top of that.

HOSTEL FEES
TIET offers both AC and non-AC hostel accommodation. Hostel fees range from
approximately Rs 52,000 to Rs 1,37,000 per year. The figure within that range
depends on:
  - Room occupancy type (single / double / triple sharing)
  - Whether the room is air-conditioned
  - Amenities such as an attached washroom

MESS FEES
Mess charges are typically in the range of Rs 40,000 to Rs 45,000 per year.

SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL AID
TIET offers tuition fee waiver scholarships ranging from 20% to 100% of the
tuition fee, depending on academic performance. Reported scholarship categories
include:
  - Merit scholarships for the top 10% of students each semester, awarded on the
    basis of academic performance.
  - Scholarships for students from economically weaker sections.
  - Additional scholarships for students with extraordinary achievements in
    sports and other co-curricular activities.

Scholarship eligibility criteria, application windows and the exact waiver
percentage for each category are published by the institute each year; students
should consult the current scholarship notification.

FEE PAYMENT AND OFFICIAL CIRCULAR
The authoritative fee chart and fee circular, including the per-programme
breakdown and payment schedule, is published at:
  https://www.thapar.edu/students/pages/fee-circular-and-fee-chart
