THAPAR INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY (TIET), PATIALA
OVERVIEW, HISTORY, ACCREDITATION AND RANKINGS
Reference document compiled from public web sources (July 2026)

IDENTITY AND HISTORY
Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET), formerly known as Thapar
University, is a private deemed-to-be university located in Patiala, Punjab, India.
It was established in 1956. The institute is commonly referred to as TIET or
Thapar University, and its official website is thapar.edu.

ACCREDITATION AND RECOGNITION
TIET Patiala is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council
(NAAC) with an A++ grade. The institute also holds accreditation and recognition
from the following bodies:
  - NBA (National Board of Accreditation)
  - ABET (Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology, USA)
  - AACSB (Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business)

NIRF RANKINGS (NIRF 2025)

CAMPUS AND LOCATION
The main TIET campus in Patiala occupies approximately 250 acres of land and
houses multiple academic schools with well-equipped facilities.
TIET operates a second campus at Dera Bassi, near Chandigarh, which houses the
LM Thapar School of Management (LMTSM). All MBA and other management programmes
are offered through LMTSM at the Dera Bassi campus.

ACADEMIC SCALE
The institute offers more than 70 specialisations across undergraduate,
postgraduate and doctoral levels.

INTERNATIONAL COLLABORATIONS
TIET maintains active international academic and research collaborations,
including partnerships with:
  - Trinity College Dublin (Ireland)
  - University of Waterloo (Canada)
  - Virginia Tech (USA)
