THAPAR INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY (TIET), PATIALA
ACADEMIC PROGRAMMES, DEPARTMENTS AND SCHOOLS
Reference document compiled from public web sources (July 2026)

ACADEMIC STRUCTURE
TIET organises its academic programmes under three types of unit:
  - 7 Schools
  - 10 Departments
  - 13 Centres
Programmes are offered at undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG) and doctoral
(PhD) levels, spanning more than 70 specialisations in total.

UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMMES (B.E. / B.Tech)
Core engineering branches offered include:
  - Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
  - Computer Engineering
  - Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  - Electrical Engineering
  - Mechanical Engineering
  - Civil Engineering
  - Chemical Engineering
  - Biotechnology
  - Biomedical Engineering
Duration of the B.E./B.Tech programme is four years (eight semesters).

TIET also offers an Integrated Engineering Programme (IEP) at the undergraduate
level, admission to which is based purely on merit in the JEE (Main) examination
together with the 10+2 eligibility requirement.

POSTGRADUATE PROGRAMMES
M.E. / M.Tech specialisations include:
  - Thermal Engineering
  - Civil Infrastructure Engineering
  - Biotechnology
  - and further specialisations across the engineering departments
M.Sc. programmes are offered in:
  - Chemistry
  - Mathematics
  - Physics
M.A. is offered with specialisation in Clinical and Counselling Psychology.
The institute also offers MBA and MCA programmes.

LM THAPAR SCHOOL OF MANAGEMENT (LMTSM)
TIET has established the LM Thapar School of Management, which offers MBA, MA and
doctoral programmes. LMTSM is located at the Dera Bassi campus near Chandigarh,
and all MBA and other management programmes are delivered there rather than at
the Patiala campus.

THAPAR SCHOOL OF LIBERAL ARTS AND SCIENCES
TIET additionally runs a School of Liberal Arts and Sciences at Patiala, offering
liberal arts and sciences programmes alongside the engineering schools.

DOCTORAL PROGRAMMES (PhD)
PhD programmes are offered in almost all specialisations of Engineering,
Technology, Management and Sciences across the various Departments and Schools of
the university. Seat availability in PhD programmes varies by department and
intake cycle; prospective applicants should check the current PhD admission
notification on the official website.
